Vidyalankar School of Information Technology: Campus, Canteens and Buildings
Vidyalankar School of Information Technology (VSIT) is an educational institution located at the Vidyalankar Campus in Wadala East, Mumbai. It is affiliated with the University of Mumbai and is part of the Vidyalankar group of educational institutions. The campus is designed to provide students with an environment that supports academic learning, practical education, extracurricular activities, and social interaction.
The Campus
The VSIT campus is known for its spacious and student-friendly environment. The campus brings together academic buildings, classrooms, laboratories, a library, recreational areas, sports facilities, food outlets, and spaces for student activities. The infrastructure is designed to combine education with activities outside the classroom.
The academic areas include classrooms and computer laboratories where students can learn both theoretical concepts and practical skills. The institute also provides modern technological facilities and internet connectivity to support students in their studies. The library serves as an important academic resource, providing students with access to books, journals, newspapers, and digital information.
Another important feature of the campus is its auditorium and seminar facilities. These spaces are used for lectures, seminars, cultural programmes, competitions, meetings, and other college events. The institute's auditorium is designed with modern audio-visual facilities and has a capacity of approximately 264 people according to an institutional report.
The campus also provides areas for sports and recreational activities. Students can participate in outdoor and indoor games, giving them opportunities to relax and develop teamwork and leadership skills. Student activity spaces further contribute to campus life by providing opportunities for clubs, cultural programmes, and social activities.
The Buildings
The buildings at Vidyalankar are designed to support different aspects of student life. Academic buildings contain classrooms, laboratories, faculty areas, and other educational facilities. The campus also includes specialized spaces for seminars, cultural activities, entertainment, and student recreation.
One distinctive feature associated with the Vidyalankar campus is its architectural design. The institute has highlighted its modern architecture and student-friendly infrastructure as important aspects of the campus environment. The campus is intended to create an atmosphere that is different from a traditional educational building by combining functional learning spaces with areas for interaction and extracurricular activities.
The institute also provides facilities designed to make the campus accessible and comfortable for students and staff. These include elevators, ramps, and other accessibility provisions. Restrooms are maintained separately for different groups of users, with provisions for people with disabilities.
The campus also has an auditorium and other multipurpose spaces. In addition, VSIT's institutional handbook describes a movie theatre on campus that can accommodate around 100 viewers and is used for special student screenings. Such facilities make the campus more than just a place for attending lectures; they create opportunities for students to participate in cultural and recreational activities.
Canteens and Cafeterias
Food is an important part of everyday college life, and the VSIT campus provides several options for students. The institute's cafeteria offers a variety of food, including South Indian, North Indian, Chinese, and Continental dishes. There are also quick-service options such as sandwiches, rolls, lunch combinations, noodles, cold coffee, and tea.
The campus has emphasized cleanliness and hygiene in its food services. The cafeteria and kitchen facilities are monitored, and institutional documents describe efforts to maintain food quality, reasonable pricing, and hygienic preparation. A food and beverages committee also works to address food-related concerns and improve the dining experience for students and staff.
Different food outlets provide students with choices during breaks and between lectures. The VSIT handbook also mentions the presence of Cafe Coffee Day on campus, along with vending machines that can provide snacks and beverages outside regular cafeteria hours.
The canteens are not only places where students eat. They are also social spaces where classmates meet, discuss assignments, relax after lectures, and interact with friends. During college events and special programmes, food-related activities and festivals may also be organized, adding to the social atmosphere of the campus.
Overall Campus Experience
The combination of academic buildings, technology-enabled classrooms, laboratories, library facilities, auditorium spaces, recreational areas, and canteens creates a well-rounded campus environment at VSIT. Students can move from classrooms to laboratories for practical learning, from the library for academic research, and to the canteen or recreational areas for relaxation and social interaction.
The campus therefore plays an important role in the overall student experience. Its buildings provide spaces for education and institutional activities, while the canteens and recreational facilities provide opportunities for friendship and relaxation. The combination of these facilities helps create a lively college atmosphere.

Vidyalankar School of Information Technology offers a campus environment that combines education, technology, recreation, and student interaction.
