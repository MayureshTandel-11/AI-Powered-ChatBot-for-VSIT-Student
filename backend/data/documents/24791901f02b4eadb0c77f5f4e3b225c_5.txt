Auditoriums, Seminars, & Recreational Amenities
* M Auditorium: A 6,000-square-foot, internationally award-winning corporate-spec grand theater space used for convocations, tech symposiums, and industrial corporate meets.
* Seminar Halls: Six fully air-conditioned, multimedia-equipped minor auditorium halls distributed across structural blocks (including the newly integrated Y-Block).
* Smart Classrooms: Non-traditional spaces leveraging centrally controlled air conditioning, interactive digital smartboards, and an integrated automated Impartus / Lecture Capture System that auto-records and indexes every session for offline study.
* The "DEN" Gaming Station: A unique, recreational zone in the "S-Block" (Student Block) featuring PlayStations, pool tables, table tennis, carrom, and a professional karaoke system.
* Food Courts & Social Hubs: Multi-canteen layout featuring indoor multi-cuisine food courts, a trendy street-side "Kappa Coffee" shop, and a dedicated mobile food van system coordinated by a student-faculty food hygiene committee.
