VSIT has multiple food and refreshment options available for students. There is a canteen on the first floor as well as a food area on the ground floor. Students can also access outlets such as Nescafé, Ribbons & Balloons, and Café Coffee Day.
In addition to these options, vending machines are available for quick snacks and beverages. Students can also purchase snacks and other required items from the stationery shop. The availability of multiple food options makes it convenient for students to get refreshments during breaks and between lectures.
