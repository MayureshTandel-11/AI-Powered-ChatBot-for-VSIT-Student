Campus Architecture & Engineering Design
The campus architecture is highly distinctive, deviating radically from traditional Indian educational structures. Designed by Planet 3 Studios Architecture, the main educational complex is engineered as an open "educational village". 
* Building Skin: Wrapped in a porous, engineered polycarbonate skin that diffuses harsh sunlight, eliminates glare, and maximizes natural day-lighting. 
* Thermal Management: Employs an oversized overarching roof canopy that shades structural blocks, encouraging natural cross-ventilation and massive energy savings. 
* Interior Promenade: Features a wide internal "learning street" with open public zones, staircases built directly into architectural cutouts, a man-sized chessboard, and an indoor table tennis arena. 
* Accessibility Tech: Fully barrier-free layout integrating KONE elevators, Braille signage, and a specialized mechanical chairlift system for temporarily injured or disabled individuals.
