VSIT provides a playground where students can participate in outdoor activities and sports. Along with outdoor facilities, the college also has a Den Area, which provides students with different indoor recreational options. The Den Area includes games such as pool, chess, table tennis, carrom, and other indoor activities.
The Den Area also has gaming PCs, providing students with an additional space for gaming and recreation. These facilities give students opportunities to relax, interact with their friends, and participate in activities apart from academics.
