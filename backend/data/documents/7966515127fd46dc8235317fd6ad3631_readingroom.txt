The reading room provides students with a quiet and focused environment for self-study, revision, assignments, and examination preparation. Computers are also available in the reading room, allowing students to work on academic tasks and access digital resources while studying.
To maintain proper records of students using the reading room, students are required to scan a QR code and fill in their details every time they enter the room. This digital system helps the college keep track of usage and maintain organized records without relying on a manual register.
