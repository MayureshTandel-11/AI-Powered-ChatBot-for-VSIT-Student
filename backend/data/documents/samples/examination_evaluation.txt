The examination system at VSIT consists of internal assessment and written examination components. For theory subjects, the total marks are divided into 25 marks for internal assessment and 25 marks for the written examination.
The 25 internal marks are calculated based on different academic activities conducted throughout the semester. These include mid-semester examinations, assignments, class tests, activities, attendance, and presentations. The marks obtained from these components are considered together and rounded to form the final 25 internal marks.
For practical subjects, students are evaluated through practical work and project-based activities. Students submit a mini-project as part of their internal assessment. The final practical examination includes components such as a practical examination, viva, and project presentation.
