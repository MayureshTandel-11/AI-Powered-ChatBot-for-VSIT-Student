Attendance at VSIT is managed through the TeachUs App, which provides a convenient digital method for recording and monitoring students' attendance. Faculty members mark attendance through the app, allowing students to keep track of their attendance for different subjects. Students can regularly check their attendance percentage and identify if they need to improve their regularity. This digital system reduces paperwork and makes attendance records easier to access and maintain. It also encourages students to be responsible about attending lectures and practical sessions.
